Can MLB payrolls buy World Series titles?

By Braden King

This project compares Major League Baseball payroll with team records, rosters, playoff results, and World Series champions. Records, rosters, and completed postseason results run from 1985 through 2025. The payroll archive runs through 2026, using Lahman/SABR salary totals through 2016 and Opening Day payrolls after 2016.

Inflation adjustment uses annual average U.S. CPI-U. Payroll ranks and tiers stay within-season comparisons.

Dataset Notes

The dataset combines Lahman/SABR team, salary, postseason series, player, and appearance tables with The Baseball Cube Opening Day payroll archive. One row in the cleaned file is one player on one MLB team roster in one season, with that team's record, payroll, playoff outcome, and World Series status attached to the row.

Records, rosters, and completed postseason results cover AL and NL seasons from 1985 through 2025. Payroll-backed team seasons run through 2025. The Baseball Cube archive adds every club's Opening Day payroll for 2017-2026; 2026 payroll is displayed separately because the project has no completed 2026 team-season outcome set yet.

Historical comparison charts can convert payroll to 2025 dollars using the Bureau of Labor Statistics' annual-average CPI-U. This changes dollar values only; payroll ranks and tiers remain within-season measures. BLS CPI-U annual averages

Computed Fields

  • Season payroll = Lahman/SABR listed-salary sum through 2016 and Opening Day contracted-player payroll from 2017-2026
  • Payroll rank = team payroll rank within that season
  • Payroll percentile = payroll rank converted to a 0-1 within-season scale
  • Winning percentage = team wins / completed regular-season decisions
  • Playoff team = appeared in Lahman postseason series or won division/wild card/pennant/title
  • World Series winner = team marked as the winner of the WS round
