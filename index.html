This project studies Major League Baseball payroll, team records, rosters, playoff results, and World Series champions from 1985 through 2025. Payroll analysis uses the reproducible salary years available in Lahman, 1985 through 2016, while records, rosters, and postseason outcomes continue through the latest season in the data.

Dataset Notes

The dataset combines Lahman/SABR team, salary, postseason series, player, and appearance tables. One row in the cleaned file is one player on one MLB team roster in one season, with that team's record, payroll, playoff outcome, and World Series status attached to the row.

Records, rosters, and postseason results cover AL and NL seasons from 1985 through 2025. Salary coverage is available from 1985 through 2016, so payroll filters and payroll charts use those seasons and mark newer seasons as payroll unavailable.

Computed Fields

  • Season payroll = sum of listed player salaries for a team-season
  • Payroll rank = team payroll rank within that season
  • Payroll percentile = payroll rank converted to a 0-1 within-season scale
  • Winning percentage = team wins / completed regular-season decisions
  • Playoff team = appeared in Lahman postseason series or won division/wild card/pennant/title
  • World Series winner = team marked as the winner of the WS round
